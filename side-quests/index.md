**I don't usually go looking for a side quest. Somehow, one finds me.**

Something catches my attention. I have one little question, which leads to another, which leads to another, and suddenly I've gone much further than I ever meant to.

That's the space Side Quests live in. They aren't things I set out to turn into projects or investigations. They're things I stumble across, get curious about, and follow just to see where they go.

Some stay small. Some get weirdly specific. And apparently, some become full projects.
